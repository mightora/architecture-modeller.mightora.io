Architecture icon collections and use terms

The searchable picker includes 44 curated icons. Artwork is bundled locally for the website, offline edition, VS Code and exports. Vendor icons identify the products represented in your architecture. Keep product names close to the artwork and preserve proportions.

  • Microsoft Azure: curated service icons from the official V24 architecture pack. Use them for the permitted architecture and documentation purposes. Do not crop, rotate, recolour or distort Microsoft product artwork.
  • AWS Architecture Icons: curated service icons from the official July 2026 package. AWS provides these assets for architecture diagrams and related technical materials. AWS retains its intellectual-property and trademark rights.
  • Lucide general architecture symbols: people, servers, databases, clouds, workflows and other technology-neutral symbols. ISC, with MIT notices for icons derived from Feather; full notices are bundled.

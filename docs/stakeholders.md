# Stakeholder mapping

A stakeholder can represent a person, a role, a team or an organisation. Use Stakeholder → Documentation for organisation, role, responsibility and ownership classification. Avoid unnecessary personal information in shared architecture files.

Business mappings → Stakeholder participates in process links a stakeholder to a process or step. Optional RACI roles are Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed. Roles are explicit on each relationship and are not inherited by nested steps.

For associations with systems, capabilities or applications, add ordinary relationship types such as owns, depends on or approves in the modelling language. RACI annotations currently apply specifically to stakeholder-to-process links; arbitrary associations remain available through generic typed relationships and entity documentation.

Select a stakeholder to inspect exact relationships or use Impact to explore reachable processes and components. Select an application to see its direct process support and potential downstream stakeholders. Reachability is descriptive and does not establish causality or operational outage impact.

The Stakeholder perspective isolates responsibility mapping. Business traceability includes supporting technical components. Both are saveable and individually exportable. Full packs retain related processes, responsibilities and exact relationship endpoints.
